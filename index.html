Common questions.

A few basics about working with CWKIT. If you have a specific requirement, send us a note.

Do you work with businesses outside India?+

Yes. CWKIT is designed to work remotely with businesses in India and international markets. Availability and delivery scope are confirmed for each engagement.

Can you provide ongoing IT support?+

Ongoing support can be discussed based on your environment, user count, coverage hours and service expectations. We agree the scope before work begins.

Do you only work with Microsoft technologies?+

Our initial specialization is Microsoft cloud and workplace technology, including Microsoft 365, Entra ID and Intune. Contact us with your requirements to discuss fit.

How does a project start?+

Start with a short introduction describing your business and the IT challenge. We can then discuss the right next step, scope and timeline.
