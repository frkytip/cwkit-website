# CWKIT — Outreach templates

Use these as starting points. Personalize the first line and mention a specific, verifiable reason for contacting the recipient. Do not send bulk unsolicited spam.

## Discovery call opening
Thanks for making time. I’d like to understand your environment and the problem first, then I can say whether CWKIT is a fit. I won’t recommend changes until we understand access, business impact and change controls.

## Proposal outline
1. Client context and problem statement
2. Objectives and measurable outcomes
3. In-scope tasks and deliverables
4. Out-of-scope items and assumptions
5. Client responsibilities and required access
6. Timeline and milestones
7. Fees, taxes, payment terms and validity
8. Change control and acceptance
9. Confidentiality, data handling and access removal
10. Sign-off
