# CWKIT — Business launch pack

## Positioning
**CWKIT — Practical IT. Confident Business.**  
Independent IT consulting focused initially on Microsoft 365, cloud identity, endpoint management and remote IT operations.

**Target market:** small and midsize businesses, and IT service providers/MSPs in India and internationally. Start with a narrow, deliverable offer rather than claiming to be a full-service global MSP from day one.

## Initial service catalogue
1. Microsoft 365 health check and administration
2. Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive configuration/support
3. Entra ID identity hardening: MFA, Conditional Access review and access hygiene
4. Intune endpoint onboarding, configuration and application deployment
5. Microsoft 365 migration planning and implementation support
6. PowerShell automation and admin reporting
7. Remote IT support / white-label MSP back-office support, only under a written scope

Do not advertise 24/7 coverage, guaranteed response times, certifications, customer results, partner status or security guarantees unless they are real and contractually supportable.

## Suggested pilot pricing (validate against actual scope)
These are starting discussion ranges, not market-rate claims or fixed quotations. Quote in the buyer's currency after scoping.

| Offer | Suggested pilot range | Scope boundary |
|---|---:|---|
| M365 environment review | ₹8,000–₹20,000 | One tenant, agreed checks, findings report; no remediation included |
| M365 setup / configuration project | ₹15,000–₹60,000+ | Depends on users, workloads, migration and complexity |
| Intune onboarding pilot | ₹12,000–₹35,000 | Limited device/user cohort and agreed policies |
| PowerShell automation task | ₹5,000–₹25,000+ | One defined script/workflow, testing and handover |
| Monthly remote admin retainer | ₹15,000–₹45,000+ | Strict hours, user/device cap, response window and exclusions |

**Pricing rule:** do not sell unlimited support for a flat low fee. Define included hours, supported users/devices, service hours, severity definitions, response targets, out-of-scope work and overage rates. Never promise resolution time when you can only control response time.

## First 30-day validation plan
### Week 1 — credibility and offer
- Complete the website placeholders and create a real business inbox.
- Prepare a one-page capability statement and a sample sanitized M365 health-check report.
- Define two fixed-scope offers: M365 health check and Intune onboarding pilot.
- Create a simple proposal / statement-of-work template.
### Week 2 — prospect list
- Identify 40–60 MSPs or businesses that visibly use Microsoft 365.
- Prioritize firms with a clear operational pain point and an identifiable decision-maker.
- Do not scrape or spam. Personalize each approach and respect opt-outs.
### Week 3 — outreach
- Send 5–10 tailored messages per business day.
- Ask for a short discovery call, not an immediate retainer.
- Track contact, date, problem hypothesis, reply, next step and outcome.
### Week 4 — close a paid pilot
- Offer a small paid diagnostic or clearly bounded implementation.
- Get written approval, access authorization, scope, payment terms and data-handling expectations before touching a tenant.
- Ask for a testimonial only after real delivery and explicit permission.

## Discovery call questions
1. How many users, devices and Microsoft 365 tenants are in scope?
2. Which workloads and licenses are in use?
3. What issue is costing the team the most time or risk today?
4. What has already been tried?
5. Who approves access and changes?
6. Is this a one-time project or recurring support need?
7. What service hours, response expectations and compliance constraints apply?
8. What budget range and decision timeline are realistic?

## Non-negotiable delivery controls
- Written authorization before accessing client tenants or devices.
- Least-privilege access; no shared passwords or personal accounts.
- MFA, secure secret handling and approved remote access.
- Change plan, rollback plan and change log for production changes.
- Backup/restore and retention assumptions documented, not implied.
- Client data confidentiality and deletion/return process.
- Signed scope, fees, payment milestones and acceptance criteria.
- No use of client names, logos, screenshots or testimonials without written permission.

## India setup checklist
This is an operational checklist, not legal or tax advice. Ask a CA/company secretary/lawyer to advise on the right structure and registrations for your facts.
- Decide whether to begin as an individual proprietor or incorporate an entity; compare liability, credibility, costs and compliance.
- Check name availability and relevant trademark/domain conflicts before investing in the brand.
- Obtain PAN and a suitable current account / payment collection setup as applicable.
- Ask a CA about GST registration, place-of-supply, export-of-services conditions, LUT (if relevant), invoicing and income-tax obligations. Do not assume every overseas invoice is automatically zero-rated.
- Prepare client MSA, SOW, NDA and data-processing terms appropriate to the engagement.
- Keep business and personal money separate and maintain invoices/expense records.
- Check local requirements and any sector/client-specific security obligations.

## Website launch blockers
- Create and test the actual mailbox before displaying it.
- Replace generic company description with the correct legal operator identity.
- Confirm whether CWKIT is a registered business name or a trading/brand name. Do not imply incorporation if not done.
- Add real phone, address, social profiles and booking link only if intended for public use.
- Review privacy/terms pages against actual processing and legal requirements.
- Connect a real form backend before promising direct form submissions.
- Configure domain DNS carefully; preserve existing MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC records.
- Test mobile, accessibility, form flow, links, metadata and domain HTTPS.
